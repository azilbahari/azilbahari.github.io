CIMAH explained

How a major-hazard safety case works

Malaysia's Control of Industrial Major Accident Hazards (CIMAH) Regulations require plants that store large amounts of dangerous substances to prove they are safe. He has contributed to more than 239 of these reports.

  1. Part A · HazardsIdentify dangerous substances and major-accident scenarios
  2. Part B · InstallationDescribe the site, process units and surroundings
  3. Part C · Safety systemSafety management, consequence modelling and risk estimates
  4. Outcome · Safer plantReport accepted by DOSH; emergency plans and risks controlled
