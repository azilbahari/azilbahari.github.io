CIMAH explained

How a major-hazard safety case works

Malaysia's Control of Industrial Major Accident Hazards (CIMAH) Regulations require plants that store large amounts of dangerous substances to prove they are safe. He has contributed to more than 239 of these reports.

  1. 01
    Part A · HazardsIdentify dangerous substances and major-accident scenarios
  2. 02
    Part B · InstallationDescribe the site, process units and surroundings
  3. 03
    Part C · Safety systemSafety management, consequence modelling and risk estimates
  4. 04
    Outcome · Safer plantReport accepted by DOSH; emergency plans and risks controlled

How this is calculated

TNT equivalence: W = η × M × 10, where M is the flammable mass in kg, 10 is roughly the ratio of hydrocarbon to TNT heat of combustion, and η is a yield set by congestion (0.03, 0.06, 0.10). Each ring radius is R = Z × W1/3, with scaled distances Z of 18, 8 and 6 m/kg1/3 for 0.07, 0.21 and 0.35 bar. All coefficients are rounded and illustrative. Real studies use validated models (for example TNO multi-energy or Baker-Strehlow-Tang) and site data.

Exposure index: for each release point, the control room scores 100 inside 0.35 bar, 50 inside 0.21 bar, 10 inside 0.07 bar; the index is the average.
