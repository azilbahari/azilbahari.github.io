Dean, Faculty of Chemical Engineering, Universiti Teknologi MARA, Shah Alam

Chemical engineer with two decades of work turning agricultural and industrial waste into fuels, adsorbents and energy-storage materials, and keeping Malaysia's major-hazard plants safe through more than 239 CIMAH safety reports. Thirteen years of academic leadership, now Dean of a faculty of five campuses and around 190 academics.

I Research themes

  1. Biomass & waste to fuel. Combustion, co-firing, torrefaction and gasification of palm and rice residues with Malaysian coal.
  2. Adsorbents for clean air. Activated carbon and xerogel adsorbents from coconut shell, palm kernel shell and empty fruit bunch for SO₂, H₂S, CO₂ and VOC capture.
  3. Sustainable materials & energy storage. Cellulose hydrogels, aerogels and biochar electrodes for flexible supercapacitors.
  4. Process safety & risk. Vapour cloud explosion and fire modelling, dynamic risk assessment and CIMAH compliance.
